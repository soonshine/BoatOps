## Gate A scope

This local candidate implements only the organization-scoped slot catalog and inventory compatibility core:

- five seed-owned presets (`FULL_DAY_8H`, `FULL_DAY_6H`, `AM_4H`, `PM_4H`, `PM_2_5H`);
- reusable custom templates and date-specific custom instances/overrides;
- `ACTIVE`, `DRAFT`, and `RETIRED` catalog state data;
- effective date ranges and all-boats or selected-boats scope;
- organization-timezone conversion of local service dates/times to UTC;
- distinct service and occupied intervals, including boat and slot-specific buffers;
- organization-scoped, auditable `ALLOW`/`DENY` compatibility rules;
- fail-closed compatibility for custom or otherwise unspecified slot pairs;
- identified-slot support in availability, HOLD creation, confirmation inheritance, and booking amendment;
- frozen slot identity, interval, name, code, and duration snapshots on HOLD/allocation/booking history;
- stable `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE`, `SLOT_COMPATIBILITY_CONFLICT`, and `SLOT_CROSSES_MIDNIGHT` business errors.

No seat-based inventory was introduced. Inventory remains one boat plus a half-open `occupied_start`/`occupied_end` interval, with active allocation overlap prevention, organization isolation, transactions, revision updates, and idempotency preserved.

## Compatibility decision

For two identified slots on the same boat and organization-local service date, an explicit `ALLOW` rule is required. A rule alone is insufficient: the calculated occupied intervals must also be non-overlapping. Operational `BLOCKED` allocations and legacy time-only allocations continue to participate in physical interval checks.

The default preset matrix denies all preset pairs. This includes the required full-day exclusions and `PM_4H`/`PM_2_5H` exclusion. `AM_4H`/`PM_4H` becomes combinable only after one canonical pair rule is changed to `ALLOW`, and only when combined boat and slot buffers still leave non-overlapping occupied intervals.

## API and contract compatibility

No route was added. The existing availability, HOLD, and amend request contracts accept either:

1. legacy `starts_at` plus `ends_at`;
2. `slot_offering_id` plus organization-local `service_date`; or
3. `custom_slot_instance_id`.

When a slot identity is present, BoatOps resolves the service and occupied intervals from organization-owned catalog data and does not use caller times as the authority. Confirmation copies the interval and identity frozen on the HOLD.

Route baseline remains **38 total routes / 20 internal Operations routes**.
